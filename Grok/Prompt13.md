**LinkedIn, Wellfound, ai-jobs.net, and AI-native company career pages** are among the strongest platforms for AI-Assisted Product Engineer jobs in 2026.

These roles typically combine product engineering (end-to-end ownership of features, user focus, and business outcomes) with heavy use of AI tools and LLMs. Engineers in these positions build AI-powered product features, agents, internal tools, or full applications—often leveraging AI coding assistants (Cursor, Claude, Copilot, etc.) for rapid shipping while maintaining strong full-stack and product sense.

### Top Platforms for AI-Assisted Product Engineer Roles
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works | Tips |
|----------|----------|--------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Volume, networking, and recruiter outreach | Highest number of “AI Product Engineer,” “AI-Assisted Product Engineer,” and related product engineering roles | Search “AI Product Engineer,” “Product Engineer AI,” or “AI-assisted full-stack.” Highlight AI tools, shipped AI features, and product ownership. |
| **Wellfound** (AngelList) | Startups & early-to-growth AI companies | Strong for AI-native and product-focused startups with equity transparency | Filter by AI/ML + Engineering/Product; many roles emphasize rapid AI-powered shipping. |
| **ai-jobs.net** | Dedicated AI/ML roles | Clean, focused board for AI engineering and product-adjacent positions | Low noise and good global coverage of AI product work. |
| **Built In** | Tech company culture & hub-based roles | Solid listings at AI-adopting tech firms | Useful for mid-size companies with strong product engineering cultures. |
| **Company Career Pages** | Highest-signal opportunities | Many specialized roles appear here first | Prioritize PostHog, Retool, OpenAI, Anthropic, and AI-native startups. |

### Other Strong Options
- **Y Combinator Work at a Startup** — Excellent for early-stage product-engineer-friendly companies that value ownership and speed.
- **Remote Vibe Coding Jobs** / **Vibehackers.io** — Relevant for the AI-assisted / rapid-prototyping aspect of these roles.
- **Indeed** and **Glassdoor** — For broader volume and salary insights.
- **Hacker News “Who’s Hiring”** threads and **YC-related boards** — High-signal for product-minded engineering roles at startups.
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace useful for experienced product engineers with AI experience.
- **Hugging Face Jobs** — Stronger for more research-oriented or open-source AI product work.

### Search Tips
- **Effective keywords**: “AI Product Engineer,” “AI-Assisted Product Engineer,” “Product Engineer – AI,” “Full-Stack AI Engineer,” “AI-native Product Engineer,” plus tools (Cursor, Claude, LangChain, RAG, agents).
- Emphasize a portfolio of shipped product features (especially AI-powered ones), user/product sense, full-stack skills, and fluency with AI coding tools.
- These roles often appear under broader titles like “Product Engineer,” “Full-Stack Engineer,” or “Software Engineer – AI Product”—read descriptions carefully for AI-assisted or product-ownership language.
- Combine LinkedIn + Wellfound + direct company pages for the best coverage, and set alerts for both exact and related titles.

This is a high-growth intersection of product engineering and AI tooling. Showcase concrete shipping examples and AI fluency on your profiles for the strongest results. Good luck!
