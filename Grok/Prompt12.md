**LinkedIn, dedicated vibe-coding job boards, Wellfound, Dice, and standard full-stack platforms** are the strongest places to find Vibe/Spec Coder (Full-Stack) jobs in 2026.

“Vibe/Spec Coder” (or simply “Vibe Coder”) is an emerging full-stack role focused on rapid development using AI coding tools (Cursor, Claude, GitHub Copilot, v0, Bolt, Lovable, etc.). These developers turn high-level specs, ideas, or “vibes” into production-ready features at high speed while maintaining solid engineering fundamentals. The title emphasizes AI-assisted, ownership-driven full-stack work rather than traditional slow, ticket-based coding.
