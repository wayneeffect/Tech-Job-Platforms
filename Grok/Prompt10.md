**LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, Glassdoor, and major technology company career pages** are among the strongest platforms for Solutions Architect jobs in 2026. These roles typically involve designing technical solutions to business problems, often customer- or stakeholder-facing, and can specialize in cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), software, enterprise systems, security, or domain-specific technologies.

### Top Platforms for Solutions Architect Roles
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works for Solutions Architects | Tips |
|----------|----------|---------------------------------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Networking, mid-to-senior/principal roles, recruiter outreach | Largest volume of Solutions Architect postings; recruiters heavily source here | Optimize profile with keywords (Solutions Architect, Cloud Solutions Architect, pre-sales, AWS/Azure/GCP, system design, customer engagement). Highlight certifications and business-outcome focus. |
| **Dice** | Specialized tech & enterprise roles, contracts | Tech-focused with strong skill-based matching | Excellent for detailed technical filters and contract-to-hire opportunities. |
| **Indeed** | High volume & broad coverage | Aggregates listings across tech companies, consultancies, and enterprises | Set alerts for “Solutions Architect,” “Solution Architect,” or specialized variants (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect). |
| **Glassdoor** | Research + jobs | Combines listings with salary data (often $180K–$300K+ total pay depending on level, company, and location) and reviews | Useful for evaluating employers like AWS, NVIDIA, Deloitte, or Salesforce. |
| **Company Career Pages** | Highest-quality & direct opportunities | Many roles are posted first (or exclusively) here | Prioritize **AWS/Amazon**, **Microsoft**, **Google**, **Salesforce**, **NVIDIA**, **Databricks**, and major consultancies (Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini). |

### Other Strong Options
- **Wellfound** — Strong for startup and scale-up Solutions Architect roles with equity transparency.
- **Built In** — Tech company culture and architecture positions in major hubs.
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace where companies approach you with salary details (effective for experienced architects).
- **Ladders** — Focused on higher-paying ($100K+) roles.
- **Remote-focused boards** — We Work Remotely, FlexJobs, Haystack, or WantRemote for fully remote Solutions Architect opportunities (many exist in this field).
- **ClearanceJobs** — Relevant for government, defense, or cleared Solutions Architect positions.

### Search Tips Specific to Solutions Architect Roles
- **Effective keywords**: “Solutions Architect,” “Solution Architect,” “Cloud Solutions Architect,” “Enterprise Solutions Architect,” “Pre-Sales Solutions Architect,” plus platform-specific terms (AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect, Salesforce Architect) and skills (system design, architecture frameworks, customer workshops, PoCs).
- Emphasize customer-facing experience, technical depth, business acumen, and certifications (AWS Solutions Architect Professional, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Google Professional Cloud Architect, TOGAF, etc.).
- Direct applications on major cloud and software vendor career pages often outperform general boards for these roles.
- Combine platforms: LinkedIn for visibility and networking + Dice/Indeed for volume + company career pages for premium openings.
- Showcase the ability to translate business requirements into technical architectures and influence stakeholders.

Solutions Architect remains a high-demand, well-compensated career path with strong remote and hybrid options. Optimize your profiles with relevant experience and certifications, set targeted alerts, and prioritize networking alongside applications. Good luck!
