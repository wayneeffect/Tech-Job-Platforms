**LinkedIn, Wellfound, Dice, and Indeed** are among the strongest platforms for Software Architect jobs in 2026. These senior roles focus on system design, scalability, microservices, cloud-native architecture, distributed systems, and technical leadership—often requiring 7–15+ years of experience.

### Top Platforms for Software Architect Roles
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works for Software Architects | Tips |
|----------|----------|--------------------------------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Networking, mid-to-senior/principal roles, recruiter outreach | Largest volume of Software Architect / Solutions Architect postings; recruiters actively source senior talent here | Optimize profile with keywords (microservices, event-driven architecture, AWS/Azure/GCP, domain-driven design, scalability, TOGAF). Enable “Open to Work” and engage in architecture groups. |
| **Wellfound** (formerly AngelList) | Startups & growth-stage companies | Strong coverage of Software Architect roles with salary/equity transparency | Filter by company stage, funding, and remote; many innovative architecture positions appear here. |
| **Dice** | Specialized tech & enterprise roles, contracts | Tech-only focus with skill-based matching; solid for architecture positions in larger organizations | Excellent for detailed technical filters and contract-to-hire opportunities. |
| **Indeed** | High volume & broad coverage | Aggregates many listings across industries | Set precise alerts and cross-check company career pages. |
| **Glassdoor** | Research + jobs | Combines listings with salary data, reviews, and interview insights | Useful for compensation benchmarking (Software Architect total pay often ranges widely, frequently $180K–$300K+ in the US depending on level and location). |

### Other Strong Options
- **Built In** — Tech company culture, local hubs, and architecture roles at mid-size to large tech firms.
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace where companies approach you with salary details upfront; strong for experienced architects.
- **Arc.dev** or similar vetted platforms — High-quality remote and freelance/contract architecture opportunities.
- **We Work Remotely / FlexJobs / Remote OK** — Good for fully remote Software Architect positions.
- **Company career pages** — Prioritize direct applications at Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Netflix, Salesforce, Uber, and major enterprises—many senior roles are posted there first.
- **Levels.fyi** — Excellent for compensation transparency and tech company leveling data related to architect roles.

### Search Tips Specific to Software Architect Roles
- **Effective keywords**: “Software Architect,” “Solutions Architect,” “Principal Software Architect,” “Enterprise Architect,” “Cloud Architect,” plus technologies (microservices, Kubernetes, event sourcing, CQRS, AWS/Azure architecture, system design, scalability patterns).
- Emphasize system design experience, technical leadership, trade-off analysis, and cross-team influence on your profile and resume.
- Combine platforms: LinkedIn for visibility and networking + Wellfound for startups + Dice/Indeed for broader reach.
- For remote or high-compensation roles, carefully filter and verify requirements (many architecture positions remain hybrid or on-site due to collaboration needs).
- Professional communities (e.g., architecture meetups, IEEE, or relevant Slack/Discord groups) and referrals often surface strong opportunities not widely posted.

Software architecture remains a high-demand, well-compensated career path. Optimize your profiles on the leading platforms, set targeted alerts, and pair applications with networking for the best results. Good luck!
