**LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice, and the major cloud providers’ career pages** (especially AWS/Amazon) are among the strongest platforms for Cloud Support jobs in 2026. These roles typically involve technical support for cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), troubleshooting, customer issue resolution, infrastructure support, and related operations—ranging from entry-level Cloud Support Associate/Engineer positions to more advanced support roles.

### Top Platforms for Cloud Support Roles
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works for Cloud Support | Tips |
|----------|----------|--------------------------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Networking, mid-level roles, recruiter outreach | High volume of Cloud Support Engineer postings; recruiters actively search here | Optimize profile with keywords (AWS Support, Azure Support, GCP, troubleshooting, CloudWatch, IAM, VPC, ITIL). Use “Open to Work” and certifications prominently. |
| **Indeed** | High volume & broad coverage | Aggregates many Cloud Support listings across companies and staffing firms | Set alerts for “Cloud Support Engineer,” “Cloud Support Associate,” or platform-specific terms. |
| **Dice** | Specialized tech/IT roles, contracts | Tech-focused board with strong coverage of cloud and support positions | Good for skill-based matching and contract opportunities. |
| **Glassdoor** | Research + jobs | Listings plus salary data (median Cloud Support Engineer total pay often ~$130K–$140K+ in the US) and company reviews | Useful for evaluating employers like AWS, Snowflake, or consulting firms. |
| **Cloud Provider Career Pages** | Highest-quality & direct opportunities | Many roles are posted first (or exclusively) here | Prioritize **AWS/Amazon Careers** (large volume of Cloud Support Engineer roles), Microsoft (Azure), Google Cloud, and Oracle Cloud. |

### Other Strong Options
- **Wellfound** — Useful for startup and scale-up cloud support or technical support roles with equity transparency.
- **Built In** — Tech company culture and cloud-related support positions in major hubs.
- **Remote-focused boards** — We Work Remotely, FlexJobs, Remote OK, or similar for fully remote Cloud Support opportunities (common in this field).
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace where companies can approach you (stronger for mid-to-senior technical support talent).
- **Staffing agencies** — Firms specializing in IT/cloud support often post contract and permanent roles on the major boards or their own sites.

### Search Tips Specific to Cloud Support Roles
- **Effective keywords**: “Cloud Support Engineer,” “Cloud Support Associate,” “Technical Support Engineer – Cloud,” “AWS Support,” “Azure Support Engineer,” “GCP Support,” plus tools (CloudWatch, Azure Monitor, troubleshooting, ticketing systems, Linux, networking basics).
- Highlight relevant certifications (AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner/SysOps, Azure Fundamentals/Administrator, Google Associate Cloud Engineer) and customer-facing troubleshooting experience.
- Direct applications on AWS, Microsoft, and Google career pages frequently yield better results than general boards alone.
- Combine platforms: LinkedIn for visibility + Indeed/Dice for volume + provider career pages for premium openings.
- Many roles emphasize customer service skills alongside technical knowledge—showcase both.

Cloud Support remains a solid entry-to-mid-level path into cloud careers with good demand and remote options. Optimize your profiles, obtain or highlight key certifications, set targeted alerts, and apply promptly. Good luck!
