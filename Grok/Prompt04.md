**LinkedIn, Wellfound, ai-jobs.net, and Dice** rank among the strongest platforms for AI Architect roles in 2026. These positions focus on designing scalable AI systems, reference architectures (e.g., RAG, agentic workflows, LLM pipelines), cloud AI platforms, enterprise integration, and governance—often requiring 7+ years of experience and strong system-design skills.

### Top Platforms for AI Architect Jobs
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works for AI Architects | Tips |
|----------|----------|--------------------------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Volume, mid-to-senior & principal roles, recruiter sourcing | Highest number of AI Architect / AI Solutions Architect postings; recruiters actively search here | Optimize profile with keywords (RAG, LLM orchestration, AWS Bedrock/SageMaker, Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI, agent frameworks, enterprise architecture). Use “Open to Work” and engage in AI/architecture groups. |
| **Wellfound** (formerly AngelList) | Startups & early-to-growth stage companies | Strong for AI-focused startups with equity transparency and innovative architecture roles | Filter by company stage, funding, and remote; salary + equity often visible. |
| **ai-jobs.net** (AI Jobs Board) | Dedicated AI/ML roles with low noise | Vertical board focused on AI, ML, and data positions globally; cleaner signal than general boards | Excellent free option for specialized AI architecture listings. |
| **Dice** | Enterprise, cloud, and contract AI architecture roles | Tech-focused with skill filters; solid coverage of AI Architect positions involving AWS/Azure AI services and regulated industries | Strong for enterprise-scale and consulting-style architecture work. |
| **Glassdoor / Indeed** | Research + high volume | Aggregated listings plus salary data, reviews, and company insights | Use for benchmarking compensation (median AI architecture roles often ~$180K–$190K+ in the US) and verifying employers. |

### Other Strong & Niche Options
- **Hugging Face Jobs** — Excellent for roles involving LLMs, open-source models, transformers, and research-adjacent architecture work.
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace where companies approach you with salary details upfront; useful for experienced AI talent.
- **Y Combinator Work at a Startup** — High-signal early-stage AI startup roles.
- **Otta / Welcome to the Jungle** — Curated tech and AI roles with good transparency (strong in US/Europe).
- **Built In** — Tech company culture and infrastructure/AI roles in major hubs.
- **Company career pages** — Direct applications at Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and major consultancies often yield better results for senior architecture positions.
- **Remote boards** — We Work Remotely, FlexJobs, or Remote OK for fully remote AI Architect opportunities (growing but still mixed with hybrid requirements).

### Search Tips Specific to AI Architect Roles
- **Effective keywords**: “AI Architect,” “AI Solutions Architect,” “Generative AI Architect,” “AI Systems Architect,” “Agentic AI Architect,” plus tech stack terms (RAG, LangChain/LlamaIndex, vector databases, AWS Bedrock, Azure AI Studio, Vertex AI, MLOps, responsible AI/governance).
- Emphasize system design experience, production AI deployments, reference architectures, cost/latency trade-offs, and stakeholder communication on your profile and resume.
- Highlight relevant certifications (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect + AI/ML specialty, Azure AI Engineer, Google Cloud Professional) when they appear in postings (they are mentioned in a minority of roles but still help).
- Combine platforms: LinkedIn for visibility and networking + ai-jobs.net/Wellfound for specialized AI listings + Dice for enterprise matches.
- Monitor big tech and frontier AI company career pages directly, as many senior roles are posted there first or exclusively.

AI architecture remains a high-demand area with strong compensation. Optimize profiles on the top platforms, set precise alerts, and pair applications with targeted networking for the best outcomes. Good luck!
