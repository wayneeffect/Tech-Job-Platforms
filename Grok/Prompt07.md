**LinkedIn, Dice, Indeed, and Glassdoor** are among the strongest platforms for System Architect jobs in 2026. These roles typically involve high-level system design, integrating software/hardware components, enterprise IT systems, infrastructure architecture, scalability, and often span technical, solutions, or enterprise architecture responsibilities (frequently in tech, defense, aerospace, or large enterprises).

### Top Platforms for System Architect Roles
| Platform | Best For | Why It Works for System Architects | Tips |
|----------|----------|------------------------------------|------|
| **LinkedIn** | Networking, senior/principal roles, recruiter outreach | Highest volume of System Architect / Systems Architect postings; recruiters actively source here | Optimize profile with keywords (system architecture, enterprise architecture, TOGAF, model-based systems engineering, cloud infrastructure, integration). Use “Open to Work” and follow relevant companies/groups. |
| **Dice** | Specialized tech & enterprise roles, contracts | Tech-focused with strong skill filters; good coverage of architecture positions | Useful for detailed technical matching and contract opportunities. |
| **Indeed** | High volume & broad coverage | Aggregates many listings across industries, including defense and enterprise | Set alerts for “System Architect,” “Systems Architect,” or “Enterprise Architect.” |
| **Glassdoor** | Research + jobs | Combines listings with salary data (median total pay often ~$200K+ in the US) and company reviews | Strong for evaluating employers like Cisco, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, or consulting firms. |
| **Wellfound** | Startups & growth-stage tech companies | Solid for modern system architecture roles with equity transparency | Filter by company stage and remote options. |

### Other Strong Options
- **Built In** — Tech company culture and architecture roles in major hubs.
- **Company career pages** — Critical for high-quality openings. Prioritize Boeing, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, NVIDIA, Cisco, major cloud providers, and large enterprises—many specialized System Architect roles appear here first.
- **Hired** — Reverse marketplace useful for experienced architects (companies approach you with salary details).
- **Remote-focused boards** — We Work Remotely, FlexJobs, or similar for hybrid/remote System Architect positions (availability varies by industry).
- **Clearance-related** — ClearanceJobs if the role requires security clearance (common in defense/aerospace systems architecture).
- **Levels.fyi** — Helpful for compensation benchmarking in tech companies.

### Search Tips Specific to System Architect Roles
- **Effective keywords**: “System Architect,” “Systems Architect,” “Enterprise Architect,” “Solutions Architect,” “Technical Architect,” “Platform Architect,” plus terms like model-based systems engineering (MBSE), TOGAF, cloud architecture, integration, or domain-specific skills (e.g., aerospace, defense, IT infrastructure).
- Highlight system-level design experience, trade-off analysis, cross-functional leadership, and relevant frameworks or tools on your profile and resume.
- Combine platforms: LinkedIn for visibility and networking + Dice/Indeed for volume + direct company pages for premium opportunities.
- Defense, aerospace, and large enterprise roles are especially common for this title—monitor those employers closely.
- Professional networks (e.g., systems engineering societies or relevant conferences) can surface referrals and unposted openings.

System Architect positions remain in solid demand with competitive compensation. Optimize your profiles on the leading platforms, set precise alerts, and pair applications with networking for the strongest results. Good luck!
